Implement bounded parallel dispatch.
- Durable READY jobs are source of truth.
- max_parallel_workers default 3.
- Dependencies must be satisfied.
- Worktree/resource exclusivity enforced.
- Jobs from separate repositories may run concurrently.
- Separate Cursor subprocess per active job.
- Graceful shutdown stops new leases first.
- Restart recovers expired leases.
- Commands: dispatch --once and dispatch --until-idle.
- Test two jobs in different repositories overlap while each remains in its own repo.
- Test dependent jobs do not start early.

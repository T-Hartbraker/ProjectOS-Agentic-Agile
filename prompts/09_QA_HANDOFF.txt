Implement automated delivery-to-assurance handoff.
- Successful DELIVERY captures exact candidate SHA.
- Create required assurance jobs in ProjectOS DB.
- Assurance worktree is from the same registered project repository.
- QA references candidate SHA and source delivery run.
- Blocking QA failure invokes project-local projectctl to create/update defect evidence.

- Stale QA cannot approve a newer SHA.
- Passing QA updates project-local evidence/traceability through projectctl.
- Add pass, fail->defect, rework->retest, stale evidence, and wrong-repository tests.
